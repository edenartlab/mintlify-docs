## 3. Google API Services and User Data

**Access and purpose.** In new Eden (`dev.eden.art` and `bp.eden.art`), connecting Google lets a permitted agent list your calendars, read/create/update/delete events, check availability and answer Calendar questions. We access your Google account ID and email; calendar names, IDs and time zones; event titles, descriptions, times, locations, attendees, responses and meeting links; and connection credentials and permissions. We do not request Gmail or calendar-sharing/settings access. Calendar use requires Google authorization, agent permission and a separate in-conversation processing agreement.

**AI processing and sharing.** With that agreement, relevant Calendar data and conversation context go to Anthropic (Claude) for your requested assistance, including follow-up context, titles and private agent notes/memory. Eden permits only approved processing routes. Anthropic does not train on API data by default; Eden's API feedback sharing and its approved Claude subscription's model improvement are disabled. API retention is normally 30 days; subscription and feature retention may differ. See [Anthropic's retention information](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention). Calendar-sensitive image, video and music processing is currently unavailable.

Calendar conversations are private. An agent owner can view only the specific messages you separately approve for that named owner—not future or changed messages, credentials or your Calendar itself. Sharing is optional; revocation stops future access but cannot retract information already viewed. Public links do not authorize disclosure. Hosting/database providers, including Neon, process data to operate Eden. Other human access is limited to security, legal or other exceptions permitted by Google's policies.

**Storage and retention.** Connections, permissions, consent and audit records are stored server-side; new Eden uses Neon's PostgreSQL database. OAuth credentials are encrypted and excluded from model-visible results. Connections to the public app and Google use HTTPS. Calendar results and derived information can remain in conversation history, private notes and agent-runtime storage; retained derived media remains private. These records, including those from retired integrations, are not automatically erased by disconnecting.

**Disconnect and deletion.** Disconnect clears usable local credentials, disables agent access and attempts Google-side revocation. You can also revoke access in [Google Account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Already-dispatched actions may finish. Disconnect does not undo event changes or delete events, history or all metadata. Request deletion of retained connection, conversation and agent-runtime data at **support@eden.art**; backups and external processors may not be erased immediately.

**Limited Use.** Eden follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including Limited Use requirements. Google data and its derivatives are used only for your requested features—not advertising, marketing, general-model training, sale or third parties' independent use. These restrictions override broader provisions elsewhere in this policy.

## 4. Retention of Information

Retention depends on the information and service feature. For Google Calendar, disconnecting removes usable credentials but does not automatically erase connection metadata, audit records or information already included in conversations. You may request deletion as described in section 3. Retention does not authorize independent reuse of Google API data or override its Limited Use restrictions.
